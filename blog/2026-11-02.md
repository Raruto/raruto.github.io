# Devlog: 2026-11-02 – 2026-11-08

That week I worked on [leaflet-city-mapper](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-city-mapper). I made it public on November 3rd and then kept pushing to it almost daily. I published [v0.0.1](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-city-mapper/releases/tag/v0.0.1) that same day, followed by [v0.0.2](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-city-mapper/releases/tag/v0.0.2), [v0.0.3](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-city-mapper/releases/tag/v0.0.3) and [v0.0.4](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-city-mapper/releases/tag/v0.0.4) over the next few days. I also pushed a couple of commits to [raruto.github.io](https://github.com/Raruto/raruto.github.io) along the way, and kept going with more commits to the plugin on November 8th.

Alongside that, I spent time in [leaflet-rotate](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate). On November 2nd I opened and merged a pull request there and published [v0.5.3](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate/releases/tag/v0.5.3). Later in the week I commented on [issue #76](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate/issues/76#issuecomment-6637080003) and on [pull request #77](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate/pull/77#issuecomment-6727274783), and I opened another pull request on November 8th.

Between the commits, I also starred a few repositories: [ol-ext](https://github.com/Viglino/ol-ext), [world-explorer](https://github.com/axieyangb/world-explorer), [wgpu](https://github.com/gfx-rs/wgpu) and [wasm-bindgen](https://github.com/wasm-bindgen/wasm-bindgen).
