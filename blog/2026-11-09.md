# Devlog: 2026-11-09 – 2026-11-15

It's been a Leaflet-heavy week for me. Most of it went into [leaflet-city-mapper](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-city-mapper), where I pushed a long run of commits and published releases [v0.0.5](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-city-mapper/releases/tag/v0.0.5), [v0.0.6](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-city-mapper/releases/tag/v0.0.6), [v0.0.7](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-city-mapper/releases/tag/v0.0.7) and [v0.0.8](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-city-mapper/releases/tag/v0.0.8). Alongside that, I closed [a bug in leaflet-rotate](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate/issues/76) about marker interactions breaking and the map center shifting after returning to bearing 0. I also merged a couple of pull requests there, and I closed [a locale-dependent number formatting issue](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/issues/319) in leaflet-elevation.

Later in the week I made a sweep through the rest of my plugin family, pushing to leaflet-kmz, leaflet-trails, leaflet-pegman, leaflet-gesture-handling, leaflet-edit-osm and a few others. I also closed [an issue about GroundOverlay alignment differing from Google Earth](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-kmz/issues/22) in leaflet-kmz, and cut [v0.2.0](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-pointable/releases/tag/v0.2.0) and [v0.2.1](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-pointable/releases/tag/v0.2.1) of leaflet-pointable.

On the upstream side, I had a pull request merged into [Leaflet/Leaflet](https://github.com/Leaflet/Leaflet) and opened another one. I also started [a discussion about bringing a WebGPU 3D engine to leaflet.js](https://github.com/Leaflet/Leaflet/discussions/10592). I even found time to review a couple of pull requests on [g3w-client](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/1009#pullrequestreview-5893050663).
