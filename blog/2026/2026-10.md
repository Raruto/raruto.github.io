# Leaflet v2 Support and a Vanilla JS Portal Proposal

*Devlog: 2026-10*

October was a month of Leaflet plugins and G3W Suite work, and it ended with a bit of a pile-up. Late in the month I opened a pull request to [add support for Leaflet v2.0.0-alpha.1](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate/pull/71) in leaflet-rotate, then did the same for [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/pull/318) and [leaflet-ui](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-ui/pull/25). Around those I opened a few leaflet-rotate PRs: [pitch rotation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate/pull/72), [Maplibre-like gestures](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate/pull/73), and a [fix for distortion when panning far from the rotation point](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate/pull/74). I also opened [a PR to Leaflet itself](https://github.com/Leaflet/Leaflet/pull/10486) proposing to add a bunch of my plugins.

![Illustration of leaflet-rotate](../img/leaflet-rotate.png)

*A current illustration of leaflet-rotate.*

Earlier in the month I put effort into tooling. On leaflet-elevation I opened PRs for [BDD testing](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/pull/315), [replacing rollup with esbuild](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/pull/312) and [removing d3js](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/pull/316). On the G3W side I opened client PRs for things like [bumping ol to 10.11.0](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/995) and [replacing vue-template-compiler](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/991). I also reviewed several PRs across g3w-client and g3w-admin.

Near the end of the month I turned to g3w-admin-portal, where I opened [a pull request converting it from a Vue 3 project to vanilla JS and CSS](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-portal/pull/24).
