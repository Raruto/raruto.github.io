# Release Notes Automation and CJS-to-ESM Cleanup

*Devlog: 2022-11*

In November I authored commits in [g3w-admin-portal](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-portal). Earlier in the month I also contributed to [g3w-admin](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin), including opening a pull request to [add a release.yml](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/414) for GitHub release notes automation.

Elsewhere in the g3w-suite, I opened a pull request on [g3w-client](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/252) to remove deprecated files and replace them with CJS to ESM imports, and another on [g3w-suite-docker](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-suite-docker/pull/84) to update Dockerfile comments. I also reviewed two g3w-suite-docker pull requests, one on [sharing common docker-compose configurations](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-suite-docker/pull/79) and one on [merging the SSL config into django.conf](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-suite-docker/pull/77).

On the Leaflet side, I authored commits in [leaflet-kmz](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-kmz) and [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation), and reviewed a [leaflet-elevation pull request](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/pull/216) covering d3, min, max, avg and precision.
