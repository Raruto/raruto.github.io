# Geocoding Checks, Project Extent Parsing and Relation Reviews

*Devlog: 2023-11*

In November I contributed to the G3W-SUITE ecosystem. I committed to [g3w-client](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client), [g3w-admin](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin), [g3w-admin-portal](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-portal), and the [editing plugin](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client-plugin-editing). I also made commits in [g3w-suite-demo-projects](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-suite-demo-projects) and [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation).

![Illustration of leaflet-elevation](../img/leaflet-elevation.png)

*A current illustration of leaflet-elevation.*

I opened a few pull requests: one that [checks for empty geocoding providers](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/525), a [code cleanup for the qplotly plugin](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client-plugin-qplotly/pull/5), and a [new default for parsing the full project extent](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/679). I also opened a [typo fix](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/675), a [docs note about extra\_hosts](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-suite-docker/pull/111), and a [version bump](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/639). On the issue side, I filed one about [auto-naming of uploaded project files](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/issues/672) and another about [handling ForeignKeyViolation](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/issues/661).

I also reviewed several pull requests, including the [Bing geocoding provider](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/505), the [WMS layers panel refactor](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/512), and [Relation Reference widget support](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client-plugin-editing/pull/73).
