# Devlog: 2024-11

In November I contributed to G3W-SUITE projects. I reviewed a pair of pull requests that move the Docker setup to Ubuntu Noble: one in [g3w-admin](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/976) updating the CI dockerfiles, and one in [g3w-suite-docker](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-suite-docker/pull/127) updating the base image to Ubuntu 24.04. Earlier in the month I opened an [issue about the SameSite attribute on the fileDownload cookie](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/issues/969) and reviewed the [pull request that fixes it](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/971).

On the client side, I opened a [pull request bumping gulp from 3.9.1 to 4.0.2](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/684) in g3w-client. I also reviewed a [bug fix for the zoom\_to\_fid URL parameter on editable layers](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/686), and authored commits in g3w-client and g3w-client-plugin-editing.

I also opened issues seeking collaborators for two Leaflet plugins, [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/issues/294) and [leaflet-rotate](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate/issues/64).
