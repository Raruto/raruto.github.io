# Devlog: 2026-09

In September I contributed to the G3W-SUITE ecosystem. I also reviewed pull requests proposing to remove \`layersstore\` across [the client](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/963) and [the admin](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/1423). I also looked at editing-related changes, like the [Add/Remove Hole in Polygon tool](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/1456) and [showing all vertices in MultiGeometry features](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/1470), plus a [refactor of the form components](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/981).

On the authoring side, I opened a few pull requests on g3w-client: a [release workflow](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/978), a [fix for sidebar positioning inside an iframe](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/976), [updated instructions for building a minimal plugin](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/977), and a [finer ordering control for context menu items](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/970). I also pushed commits to g3w-admin, g3w-client, the editing plugin, and g3w-admin-panoramax.

The month closed with two new issues: finding a way to [deprecate moment.js](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/issues/987) in the client, and [adding g3w-admin v4.0 support](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-ps-timeseries/issues/12) to the timeseries plugin. Elsewhere, I reviewed a [marker height fix](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/pull/310) in leaflet-elevation and committed to that repo and to my own site.
