# Static Editing Plugin and Search Option Pull Requests

*Devlog: 2025-08*

In August I contributed to g3w-suite projects. On the code side, I authored commits in [g3w-admin-processing](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-processing), [g3w-admin](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin), [g3w-admin-portal](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-portal) and [g3w-client](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client).

I also opened a few pull requests: one to [convert the editing plugin into a static plugin.js](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/1184), one on the [search option "Alternative Unique Level"](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/839), and a [spinner color change](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-portal/pull/16) in the portal.

A good part of the month went to reviewing other people's work. That included [pagination controls on multi-layer search](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/844), [handling layer style within editing forms](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client-plugin-editing/pull/166), a [feature locked warning](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client-plugin-editing/pull/167) in the editing plugin, and [duplicate env variables](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-suite-docker/pull/145) in the Docker setup. I also reviewed the [new sidebar\_collapse option](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/1175) in g3w-admin.
