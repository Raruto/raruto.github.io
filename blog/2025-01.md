# Proposing esbuild and Native ES6 Modules

*Devlog: 2025-01*

In January I contributed to [g3w-client](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client). I opened a [pull request to replace babel and browserify with esbuild](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/702), and a companion [pull request in the editing plugin to remove lodash and use native ES6 modules](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client-plugin-editing/pull/145). I also reported an [issue about the wrong image appearing in a modal](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/issues/703) and opened a [pull request to ensure unique IDs for modal galleries](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/704). Earlier in the month I opened a [fix for downloading relations that excludes the pdf format](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/701).

I also reviewed two pull requests on the client: [one about other layers disappearing after selecting a feature](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/699) and [one adding a Portuguese translation](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/697).

Around the rest of the g3w-suite projects, I opened issues about Django 4 support, in [g3w-admin-portal](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-portal/issues/15) and in [g3w-admin-frontend](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-frontend/issues/7). I also authored commits in g3w-admin-portal, g3w-client, and g3w-client-plugin-editing.
