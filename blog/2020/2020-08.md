# Elevation, KMZ, and UI Work Continues

*Devlog: 2020-08*

In August I contributed to [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation), [leaflet-kmz](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-kmz) and [leaflet-ui](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-ui), alongside other Leaflet plugins.

In elevation, I added an [AlmostOver example](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/commit/5c48b54984586d771f8ec3c3b217628ae03d735a) and later worked on [native support for the plugin](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/commit/11d1445864e0a549c0769b561cbc4b3ce7b54884). The current illustration below shows the relationship at the heart of the project: a route on the map and its profile beside it.

The [elevation retrospective]({{ '/blog/2018-11-02-leaflet-elevation-project.html' | relative_url }}) links to current demonstrations of that pairing.
