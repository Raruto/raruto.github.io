# Keeping Local Checkouts Local

*Contributions: 2020-01*

Looking back to January 2020, WordPress.org lists me among the contributors to [Local Development](https://wordpress.org/plugins/local-development/), a plugin by Andy Fragen for marking plugins and themes as being under local development.

The plugin's 2.5.0 changelog, dated January 23, 2020, credits me for automatically adding version-control checkouts. A handy little bridge between the files I'm working on and the WordPress admin screen.
