# Failsafe i18n and Restricted Content Redirects

*Devlog: 2025-09*

In September I contributed to G3W-SUITE projects. In [g3w-admin](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin), I authored a couple of commits and reviewed two pull requests: one on [JWT authentication for the editing REST API](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/1208) and another on [Bookmarks](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/1204).

I also opened two pull requests of my own. One is [Failsafe i18n](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-processing/pull/21) in g3w-admin-processing, where I authored a few commits. The other is [Handle restricted content (login redirect)](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-portal/pull/17) in g3w-admin-portal, alongside two commits there.

On the client side, I reviewed pull requests in [g3w-client](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client), including [zooming to features on mobile](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/852), [showing search values in bold](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/845), and the [select all button state](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/854) across multiple queries.
