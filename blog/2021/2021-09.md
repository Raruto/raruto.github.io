# Leaflet Elevation Commits

*Devlog: 2021-09*

In September I returned to [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation). The commit history points to work on two reported questions: [duplicate waypoint markers](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/issues/120) and [documentation for summary and ruler options](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/issues/118).

Those details matter when a route has both a line on the map and information in a profile. The [project retrospective]({{ '/blog/2018-11-02-leaflet-elevation-project.html' | relative_url }}) describes the plugin and links to current examples; the illustration below is current as well.
