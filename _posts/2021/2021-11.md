# Leaflet Rotate, UI and a Cockpit Stale Config Proposal

*Devlog: 2021-11*

In November I contributed to several Leaflet projects, including [leaflet-rotate](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate) and [leaflet-ui](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-ui). A reported problem in rotate made markers draggable by default; I [changed the interaction check](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate/commit/f559bda93b0299cfdf4c3c8d4b97176307d62fb7) so it respected whether dragging was enabled.

![Illustration of leaflet-rotate](../img/leaflet-rotate.png)

*A current illustration of leaflet-rotate.*

Elsewhere, I proposed a [stale-issue configuration](https://github.com/agentejo/cockpit/pull/1473) for Cockpit. It would label inactive issues and pull requests and eventually close stale issues; the proposal was not merged. I also made commits in [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation) and my [site](https://github.com/Raruto/raruto.github.io).
