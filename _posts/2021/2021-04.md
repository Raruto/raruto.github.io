# Cockpit CMS Add-on Release and Packaging Issues

*Devlog: 2021-04*

In April I authored a commit in [Raruto/leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation) and opened issues on Cockpit CMS add-ons from Paulo Gomes.

![Illustration of leaflet-elevation](../img/leaflet-elevation.png)

*A current illustration of leaflet-elevation.*

In [CockpitCMS-UserFlood](https://github.com/pauloamgomes/CockpitCMS-UserFlood/issues/3), I opened an issue suggesting a draft of a new release on GitHub for v1.0.0. I opened two more issues about publishing on Packagist.org, one for [CockpitCMS-Moderation](https://github.com/pauloamgomes/CockpitCMS-Moderation/issues/27) and one for [CockpitCMS-Autosave](https://github.com/pauloamgomes/CockpitCMS-Autosave/issues/3).

The issues were requests to make the add-ons easier to find and install, not releases I published myself. They sit alongside my own [Cockpit packages]({{ '/blog/2021-01-29-cockpit-forms-packages.html' | relative_url }}) from earlier that year.
