# Leaflet Updates and Cockpit Add-on Pull Requests

*Devlog: 2021-02*

In February I authored commits in [cockpit-blog](https://github.com/Raruto/cockpit-blog) and [leaflet-rotate](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate), as well as [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation), [leaflet-gesture-handling](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-gesture-handling), [leaflet-ui](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-ui) and [raruto.github.io](https://github.com/Raruto/raruto.github.io). I also reviewed a pull request on leaflet-elevation, [renaming the .hidden class to .leaflet-hidden](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/pull/92).

![Illustration of leaflet-rotate](../img/leaflet-rotate.png)

*A current illustration of leaflet-rotate.*

I also sent a handful of pull requests to other Cockpit projects. I opened one on the core repo to [fix Lexy's @include printing a stray 1](https://github.com/agentejo/cockpit/pull/1417), and a few for Paulo Gomes' addons: [composer installation instructions for Autosave](https://github.com/pauloamgomes/CockpitCMS-Autosave/pull/2), [improved lockout rules in UserFlood](https://github.com/pauloamgomes/CockpitCMS-UserFlood/pull/2), and [precompiled binaries for ImageOptimizer](https://github.com/pauloamgomes/CockpitCMS-ImageOptimizer/pull/1). I also opened a [BetterSlugs heading update](https://github.com/pauloamgomes/CockpitCMS-BetterSlugs/pull/11) and composer instructions for [Moderation](https://github.com/pauloamgomes/CockpitCMS-Moderation/pull/25).

MAn illustrationps://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate) project adds rotation to Leaflet maps, building initially on [Iván Sánchez Ortega's work](https://github.com/Leaflet/Leaflet/tree/rotate). Its README describes bearing and pitch options, and the [demo](https://raruto.github.io/leaflet-rotate/examples/leaflet-rotate.html) shows the controls in use.

On February 15 I also published two small Cockpit CMS examples on Packagist: [Sample Addon](https://packagist.org/packages/raruto/cockpit-sample-addon), which adds a `/api/test` endpoint, and [Sample Data](https://packagist.org/packages/raruto/cockpit-sample-data), which imports data during installation. Their READMEs link to [cockpit-blog](https://github.com/Raruto/cockpit-blog) and list compatibility with Cockpit 0.11.2.
