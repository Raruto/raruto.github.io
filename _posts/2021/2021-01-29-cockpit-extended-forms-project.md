# Giving Cockpit Forms More Room to Grow

*Projects: 2021-01*

My [Extended Forms](https://github.com/Raruto/cockpit-extended-forms) repository dates back to January 29; its [Packagist package](https://packagist.org/packages/raruto/cockpit-extended-forms) followed that day. It extends Cockpit CMS forms with uploaded-file handling and changes around opening, closing and submitting forms.

The README links the add-on to my Cockpit proposals for [form submission](https://github.com/agentejo/cockpit/pull/1399) and [file handling](https://github.com/agentejo/cockpit/pull/1411). Those proposals were not merged, but the add-on gave the ideas a separate place to live. Its documented compatibility is with Cockpit 0.11.2, so check the version before trying it today.
