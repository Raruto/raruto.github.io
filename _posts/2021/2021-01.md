# Cockpit Pull Requests, Forms, and Add-on Commits

*Devlog: 2021-01*

In January I opened pull requests on [Cockpit](https://github.com/agentejo/cockpit), including a fix for the [csfr to csrf rename](https://github.com/agentejo/cockpit/pull/1392), a [routing script for the built-in PHP server](https://github.com/agentejo/cockpit/pull/1395), and [simple phpunit tests](https://github.com/agentejo/cockpit/pull/1406). I also sent in a few changes around forms, like [cleaner submit logic](https://github.com/agentejo/cockpit/pull/1399) and [delegating data validation to Forms::submit](https://github.com/agentejo/cockpit/pull/1411). Along the way I opened two issues, one about an [unused $mailer property](https://github.com/agentejo/cockpit/issues/1409) and one asking whether to [drop the \_\_mailsubject argument](https://github.com/agentejo/cockpit/issues/1410).

I also made commits in my own repositories, including [cockpit-blog](https://github.com/Raruto/cockpit-blog), plus [cockpit-extended-forms](https://github.com/Raruto/cockpit-extended-forms), [cockpit-native-lazy-loading](https://github.com/Raruto/cockpit-native-lazy-loading), [cockpit-honeypot](https://github.com/Raruto/cockpit-honeypot), [raruto.github.io](https://github.com/Raruto/raruto.github.io) and [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation).

At the end of the month I opened two pull requests on [CockpitCMS-BetterSlugs](https://github.com/pauloamgomes/CockpitCMS-BetterSlugs): one for [Composer installation](https://github.com/pauloamgomes/CockpitCMS-BetterSlugs/pull/9) and one for a [wrong plugin folder name](https://github.com/pauloamgomes/CockpitCMS-BetterSlugs/pull/10).
