# Cockpit Pull Requests, Forms, and Add-on Commits

*Devlog: 2021-01*

In January I opened several pull requests on [Cockpit](https://github.com/agentejo/cockpit): correcting the [csfr-to-csrf rename](https://github.com/agentejo/cockpit/pull/1392), adding [routing for PHP's built-in server](https://github.com/agentejo/cockpit/pull/1395), proposing [simple PHPUnit tests](https://github.com/agentejo/cockpit/pull/1406), and changing form submission and validation ([#1399](https://github.com/agentejo/cockpit/pull/1399), [#1411](https://github.com/agentejo/cockpit/pull/1411)). I also asked about an [unused `$mailer` property](https://github.com/agentejo/cockpit/issues/1409) and whether to [drop the `__mailsubject` argument](https://github.com/agentejo/cockpit/issues/1410).

I also made commits to my [site](https://github.com/Raruto/raruto.github.io) and [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation) projects.

On January 29 I published three Cockpit CMS add-ons on Packagist. [Extended Forms](https://packagist.org/packages/raruto/cockpit-extended-forms) adds uploaded-file handling and changes to the form lifecycle; [Honeypot](https://packagist.org/packages/raruto/cockpit-honeypot) validates a hidden field and excludes invalid submissions from saved entries, as one measure against unwanted submissions; [Native Lazy Loading](https://packagist.org/packages/raruto/cockpit-native-lazy-loading) adds the browser's `loading="lazy"` attribute to images in TinyMCE and HTML editor content. All three list compatibility with Cockpit 0.11.2, so check versions before using these historical packages with a newer installation.
