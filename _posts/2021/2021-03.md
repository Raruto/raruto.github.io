# Draws on canvas

*Devlog: 2021-03*

In March I contributed to [CockpitCMS-UserFlood](https://github.com/pauloamgomes/CockpitCMS-UserFlood) and [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation): one project concerns a Cockpit add-on, the other a map's elevation profile.

The elevation history includes [adding a Canvas renderer](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/commit/d808546cbd286d063a1f00ca4925b8afbc1cafb6) and [refactoring chart resizing and component registration](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/commit/7c0262cf1f6fce0a46cbbf345c5a09745b282903). The current illustration below shows the kind of map-and-chart pairing those changes support.

For a broader view of the plugin, see the [elevation project article](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation).
