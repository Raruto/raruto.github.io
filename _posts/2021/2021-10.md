# Leaflet Elevation and KMZ Commits

*Devlog: 2021-10*

In October I contributed to [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation) and [leaflet-kmz](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-kmz). One displays a track profile; the other loads KML and KMZ data into a map.

On the file-loading side, a [KMZ commit](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-kmz/commit/1b2ceed5ce4c040db19ada7cf7817135ec2ba0b7) addressed non-UTF-8 decoding and network 404 errors. The [KMZ project article]({{ '/blog/2019-04-14-leaflet-kmz-project.html' | relative_url }}) links to examples, while the image below illustrates the elevation plugin as it appears today.
