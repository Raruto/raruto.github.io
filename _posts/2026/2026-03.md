# A Custom x-select Proposal for the Client

*Devlog: 2026-03*

March was a busy month across the G3W-SUITE repositories. In [g3w-client](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client), I opened a handful of pull requests. One [replaces the multi-layer selection in the add layer modal with a table element](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/893). Another [changes the measure control to show meters instead of km](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/891). A third [removes the CSS rules tied to Bootstrap tooltips](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/892).

The bigger thread was [starting to migrate the built-in v-select2 to a custom HTMLElement, x-select](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/902). Later I opened a [follow-up to fix Vue reactivity on the multi select](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/911). I also reviewed a couple of other client pull requests, including [loading the minified Vue library](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/908) and a [bump of ol to 10.8.0](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/910).

On the portal side, I opened pull requests for [new i18n languages](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-portal/pull/18) and a [dynamic fallback language](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-portal/pull/19), and kept committing there through the month. I also reviewed the [Qplotly multi plot pull request](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/1319) in g3w-admin.
