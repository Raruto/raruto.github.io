
# Let me BDD

*Devlog: 2026-10*

October was a month of modernization, tooling cleanups, and "true to the title" embracing Behavior-Driven Development across my Leaflet plugins.

Early on, I focused heavily on refactoring the testing and build setup for `leaflet-elevation`. I opened a major PR to introduce [BDD testing](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/pull/315), migrating the test suite from `node:test` to `cucumber-js` to make test scenarios readable and close out a long backlog of old issues. Alongside that, I sped up the build pipeline by [replacing rollup with esbuild](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/pull/312) and simplified the codebase by [removing d3js](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/pull/316) to make the project more accessible to contributors. I also brought `esbuild` and BDD tests over to [leaflet-ui](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-ui/pull/25).

Later in the month, the focus shifted to preparing for the next major Leaflet release. I opened pull requests to add support for Leaflet `v2.0.0-alpha.1` in [leaflet-rotate](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate/pull/71), [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/pull/318) (while retaining `v1.9.4` compatibility), and [leaflet-ui](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-ui/pull/25). I also opened [a PR to Leaflet itself](https://github.com/Leaflet/Leaflet/pull/10486) proposing to add several of my long-maintained plugins to the official directory.

![Illustration of leaflet-rotate](../img/leaflet-rotate.png)

*An illustration of leaflet-rotate.*

Between v2 updates, `leaflet-rotate` got some exciting new features and fixes: [pitch rotation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate/pull/72), [Maplibre-like gestures](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate/pull/73), and a [fix for distortion when panning far from the rotation point](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate/pull/74).

On the G3W-SUITE side, I kept dependencies moving forward with client PRs for [bumping OpenLayers to 10.11.0](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/995) and [replacing vue-template-compiler](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/991), reviewed several PRs across `g3w-client` and `g3w-admin`, and opened a PR to convert `g3w-admin-portal` [from a Vue 3 project to vanilla JS and CSS](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-portal/pull/24).
