# DeepWiki Badges, isMobile Refactor and Awesome List Entries

*Devlog: 2026-05*

In May I contributed to G3W-SUITE projects. On the 20th I opened a batch of pull requests adding a DeepWiki badge to [g3w-client](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/931), [g3w-admin](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/1376), [g3w-admin-frontend](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-frontend/pull/12), and a few others. Alongside those, I opened PRs removing the issue and PR templates, for example in [g3w-admin-processing](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-processing/pull/27). I also opened a [refactor of the isMobile detection logic](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/930) in the client.

Reviews were a regular part of the month too. I reviewed the client PR [adding the otherquerylayerids parameter](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/927) to multi-layer search, the [Spanish language additions](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/1367) on the admin side, and the [mobile sidebar cropping fix](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/935). I also looked at the [ResizeObserver change](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/924) and the [2FA documentation PR](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-suite.github.io/pull/59).

Outside the main suite, I opened a PR to [add G3W-SUITE](https://github.com/italia-opensource/awesome-italia-opensource/pull/196) to the awesome-italia-opensource list, and another to [add Node v22 support](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/pull/307) in leaflet-elevation. Commits also went into g3w-client, g3w-admin, and the organization's .github repository.
