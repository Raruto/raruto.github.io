# Reviewing French Translations in g3w-client

*Devlog: 2026-02*

In February I reviewed a [French translation update](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/881) in [g3w-client](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client). The author described reviewing the existing translations and correcting some of them; the pull request was merged on February 5.

My role in this entry was the review, not authorship of the translations. It's a small example of the work around a mapping interface that doesn't involve drawing a map at all.
