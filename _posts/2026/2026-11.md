# A Leaflet Map with Another Dimension

*Devlog: 2026-11*

What if a Leaflet map had a little more depth?

On November 3, I open-sourced [leaflet-city-mapper](https://github.com) with an initial push of 52 commits, iterating quickly up to [v0.0.8](https://github.com/releases/tag/v0.0.8). Exploring projects like [wgpu](https://github.com) and [wasm-bindgen](https://github.com) along the way.

I built a lean WebGPU renderer packaged into a standard `L.Layer`. It supports extruded GeoJSON, glTF models, raster/vector sources, and terrain—you can check it out in the [Paris](https://github.io), [ancient Rome](https://github.io), and [Matterhorn](https://github.io) demos.

![Illustration of leaflet-city-mapper](../img/leaflet-city-mapper.png)

In parallel, I pushed a round of updates across my Leaflet ecosystem:

- **[leaflet-rotate](https://github.com)**: Shipped v0.5.3 and v0.5.4 with fixes for `maxBounds` and [panInside after resetting bearing to 0](https://github.com/pull/77), plus a [PR adding Leaflet v2.0.0-beta.3 support](https://github.com/pull/78).
- **Other plugins**: Updated `elevation`, `pegman`, `pointable` ([v0.2.0](https://github.com) / [v0.2.1](https://github.com)), `kmz`, and `trails`, alongside a couple of PR reviews on [g3w-client](https://github.com).
- **Upstream Leaflet**: Opened a [PR to add my plugins](https://github.com) to the official list and started a [discussion on bringing a WebGPU 3D engine to Leaflet](https://github.com).
