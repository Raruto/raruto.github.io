# A Leaflet Map with Another Dimension

*Devlog: 2026-11*

What if a Leaflet map had a little more depth?

On November 3, I open-sourced [leaflet-city-mapper](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-city-mapper) with an initial push of 52 commits, iterating quickly up to [v0.0.8](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-city-mapper/releases/tag/v0.0.8). Exploring projects like [wgpu](https://github.com/gfx-rs/wgpu) and [wasm-bindgen](https://github.com/wasm-bindgen/wasm-bindgen) along the way.

I built a lean WebGPU renderer packaged into a standard `L.Layer`. It supports extruded GeoJSON, glTF models, raster/vector sources, and terrain.

You can check it out in the [Paris](https://raruto.github.io/leaflet-city-mapper/examples/leaflet-city-mapper.html), [ancient Rome](https://raruto.github.io/leaflet-city-mapper/examples/leaflet-city-mapper-rome.html), and [Matterhorn](https://raruto.github.io/leaflet-city-mapper/examples/leaflet-city-mapper-matterhorn.html) demos.

In parallel, I pushed a round of updates across my Leaflet ecosystem:

- **[leaflet-rotate](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate)**: Shipped v0.5.3 and v0.5.4 with fixes for `maxBounds` and [panInside after resetting bearing to 0](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate/pull/77), plus a [PR adding Leaflet v2.0.0-beta.3 support](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate/pull/78).
- **Other plugins**: Updated `elevation`, `pegman`, `pointable` ([v0.2.0](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-pointable/releases/tag/v0.2.0) / [v0.2.1](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-pointable/releases/tag/v0.2.1)), `kmz`, and `trails`, alongside a couple of PR reviews on [g3w-client](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/1009).
- **Upstream Leaflet**: Opened a [PR to add my plugins](https://github.com/Leaflet/Leaflet/pull/10593) to the official list and started a [discussion on bringing a WebGPU 3D engine to Leaflet](https://github.com/Leaflet/Leaflet/discussions/10592).
