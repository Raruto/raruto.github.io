# Elevation Chart Profiles and Gesture Handler Toggles

*Devlog: 2022-05*

In May I authored commits in [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation) and opened a [pull request](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/pull/199) there about handling \`distance: false\` so that the other chart profiles show up. I also pushed more commits to the same repository later in the month.

![Illustration of leaflet-elevation](../img/leaflet-elevation.png)

*A current illustration of leaflet-elevation.*

Around the end of May I moved on to [leaflet-gesture-handling](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-gesture-handling) and [leaflet-ui](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-ui). In leaflet-gesture-handling I opened a [pull request](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-gesture-handling/pull/18) to toggle map handlers only when the related options are true. In between, I also committed to [ol-vue-example](https://github.com/Raruto/ol-vue-example) and to my [GitHub Pages site](https://github.com/Raruto/raruto.github.io).
