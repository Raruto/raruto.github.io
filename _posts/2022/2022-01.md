# More Leaflet Elevation Commits

*Devlog: 2022-01*

In January I worked on [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation), including [waypoint labels, icons and positioning](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/commit/5b86a80ae7ee94c4988f601b0cca93588f470a9b). A later [change added a timestamps option](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/commit/f677fd035a19e2f9ba57c564902e3ccee3850c2b) and refactored focus-label code.

Waypoints and labels give a route more context than the shape of the elevation line alone. The illustration below shows the plugin today; the [project page](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation) links to current examples.

The January changes touched both how those points are presented and the information available around the profile.
