# A UMD Definition Proposal for Leaflet.fullscreen

*Devlog: 2022-04*

In April I authored commits in [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation), [leaflet-gesture-handling](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-gesture-handling) and [leaflet-ui](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-ui).

![Illustration of leaflet-elevation](../img/leaflet-elevation.png)

*A current illustration of leaflet-elevation.*

I also reached outside my own repositories. In [brunob/leaflet.fullscreen](https://github.com/brunob/leaflet.fullscreen) I authored a commit and opened a pull request, [Update UMD definition](https://github.com/brunob/leaflet.fullscreen/pull/103).

The report described a `root`-undefined error in a module-loading example and extra configuration needed by bundlers. The [proposal](https://github.com/brunob/leaflet.fullscreen/pull/103) adjusted the UMD definition and was merged later that month. It was a compatibility detail rather than a new map control, but one that affected how the control could be loaded.
