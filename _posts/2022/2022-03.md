# Rotate Plugin Listing and GPX Extensions in togeojson

*Devlog: 2022-03*

In March I authored commits in [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation). Alongside it, I made commits to [leaflet-ui](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-ui) and [leaflet-rotate](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate), and I also authored a commit in [Leaflet](https://github.com/Leaflet/Leaflet) itself.

![Illustration of leaflet-elevation](../img/leaflet-elevation.png)

*A current illustration of leaflet-elevation.*

I also reached out to a couple of other projects. On March 30 I opened a pull request to [add leaflet-rotate to the Leaflet plugins list](https://github.com/Leaflet/Leaflet/pull/8096). Earlier in the month, I opened a [pull request on togeojson](https://github.com/placemark/togeojson/pull/61) about handling other GPX extensions, including Garmin and custom ones, and saving them within the coordinateProperties. On March 27 I also opened an [issue on the same project](https://github.com/placemark/togeojson/issues/73) about publishing the various converters as separate components.
