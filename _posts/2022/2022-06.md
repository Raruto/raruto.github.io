# Proposing Dev Workflow Changes for g3w-client

*Devlog: 2022-06*

In June I proposed development workflow changes for [g3w-client](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client). I opened a batch of pull requests there: a [loopback default host](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/23) in the config template, [npm-version support](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/28), a [Dockerized development setup](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/29), an [updated minimum NodeJS version](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/30), and refactors of [.gitignore](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/31) and [gulpfile.js](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/32). I also opened issues about [git tags and semantic versioning](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/issues/27) and the [engines property](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/issues/26) in package.json, and one in g3w-admin about [removing yarn](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/issues/352).

On June 9 I opened a larger [ES Modules refactoring pull request](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/35) that also updates gulp and other dev dependencies. I reviewed a related [refactoring pull request](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/34) the same day, and came back to review #35 on June 15.

Alongside that, I made commits to several of my Leaflet projects, including [leaflet-ui](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-ui), [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation), [leaflet-kmz](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-kmz) and [leaflet-gesture-handling](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-gesture-handling). On June 15 I also opened two issues in g3w-suite-docker, covering a [database startup error](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-suite-docker/issues/45) and a [missing root role error](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-suite-docker/issues/44).
