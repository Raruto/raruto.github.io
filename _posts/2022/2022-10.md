# A Proposal to Remove http-proxy and G3W-SUITE Reviews

*Devlog: 2022-10*

Late October had me moving between a few corners of the G3W-SUITE ecosystem. In [g3w-client](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client), I opened a [pull request to remove the "http-proxy" dependency](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/251) from package.json, and I authored a couple of commits there too. I also authored a commit in [the sidebar plugin](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client-plugin-sidebar) and four in [g3w-suite-docker](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-suite-docker).

I reviewed pull requests on [layer categories in the map legend](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/108), [copy and paste of feature attributes](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/250), a [dropdown search field fix in the sidebar panel](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/240), and [event handlers for undefined setter methods](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/243). I also reviewed an [update to the g3w-admin README and docs images](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/392).

On the [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation) side, I opened two pull requests: one to [allow translating the small legend item switcher](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/pull/220) and another to [fix the minimum speed being 0](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/pull/219).
