# Letting L.GpxGroup Carry GeoJSON Tracks

*Devlog: 2022-12*

In December I contributed to g3w-suite projects. I authored commits in [g3w-admin-portal](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-portal) and [g3w-admin-authjwt](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-authjwt), and in the [g3w-admin](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin) repository I opened a [pull request](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/423) to apply the same code style to the `urls.py` and `apiurls.py` routes. I also opened an [issue](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/issues/431) proposing a Makefile to automate common command-line tasks.

I also authored commits in the [makefiles](https://github.com/g3w-suite/makefiles) repository. I also reviewed a [g3w-client pull request](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/273) about the develop instructions in the README.

Outside g3w-suite, I authored commits in [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation) and opened a [pull request](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/pull/228) proposing support for geojson tracks in the `L.GpxGroup` library.
