# Say no to ClickOps

*Devlog: 2024-06*

In June I contributed to g3w-suite projects. On the [g3w-admin](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin) side, I opened a pull request to [allow creating projects from the CLI](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/881) and another to [replace wait-for-it with pg\_isready](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/879) in the setup script. I also opened an issue about [replacing the built-in file manager](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/issues/880), and another about an [Internal Server Error with QueryBuilder IN / NOT IN clauses](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/issues/874). I authored commits there as well.

Around the deployment and demo tooling, I authored commits in [g3w-suite-docker](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-suite-docker) and [g3w-suite-demo-projects](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-suite-demo-projects), and opened a pull request to [restore backups from the demo projects](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-suite-docker/pull/121). I also reviewed a [PostGIS image bump](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-suite-docker/pull/120).

On the front-end and portal side, I authored commits in [g3w-client](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client), [g3w-client-plugin-editing](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client-plugin-editing) and [g3w-admin-portal](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-portal), where I opened a [pull request about the project thumbnail fallback](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-portal/pull/12). I also reviewed a [g3w-client fix](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/641) and a [website update](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-suite.github.io/pull/34).
