# G3W-Suite Refactors, Reviews and Backlog Issues

*Devlog: 2024-03*

In March I contributed to g3w-suite projects. In [g3w-client](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client) I authored a handful of commits and reviewed several pull requests, including the [refactor of G3WObject, Panel and Component classes](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/574), the [showmarker and iframetype URL parameters](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/603), a fix to [prevent an HTTP request loop](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/597) in the panoramic map control, and a [Polish translation](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/595).

On the admin side, I opened the [internal components refactor pull request](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/787) in g3w-admin and later reviewed it. I also opened issues there, such as the [missing dependency section in the search widget](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/issues/784), a [cookie SameSite attribute warning](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/issues/791), and a [JSON response that outputs "NULL" instead of null](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/issues/794).

I also opened a batch of issues in the [g3w-suite backlog](https://github.com/g3w-suite/backlog), covering topics like an [alert system based on a date field](https://github.com/g3w-suite/backlog/issues/30), [LDAP user association](https://github.com/g3w-suite/backlog/issues/29), an [iframe request](https://github.com/g3w-suite/backlog/issues/27), and [Google Tiles, Directions and Isochrones APIs](https://github.com/g3w-suite/backlog/issues/19).
