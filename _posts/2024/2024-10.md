# Query Response and Navbar Refactors, Plus Widget Reviews

*Devlog: 2024-10*

In October I contributed to g3w-suite projects. In [g3w-client](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client), I opened pull requests to [refactor the query response](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/666) and to [refactor the navbar and context menu](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/679), and I authored a handful of commits there along the way. Over in the editing plugin, I opened a [pull request](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client-plugin-editing/pull/134) to give datatable headings a "nowrap" style.

I also reviewed several pull requests. In g3w-client, I reviewed pull requests on [showing labels for the checkbox widget](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/669), [multi-value selection in value relation widgets](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/673), and [editing layer visible status](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/674), plus its [editing plugin counterpart](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client-plugin-editing/pull/133). I also reviewed [value relation ordering](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client-plugin-editing/pull/132) in the plugin.

On the admin side, I opened an [issue about exporting CSV with geometry](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/issues/963), and reviewed the related [geometry column pull request](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/965) as well as [another one](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/966) touching the attributes table layer property.
