# Formatted Values, Portal Version and Pull Request Reviews

*Devlog: 2024-04*

In April I contributed to G3W-SUITE projects. I committed to [g3w-admin](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin), [g3w-client](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client) and [g3w-admin-portal](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-portal), and I opened [a pull request](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-portal/pull/9) in the portal repo to show the portal version within the HTML source.

I also opened a few issues. One was about [sorting formatted values](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/issues/806) in the vector API response, another about [autocomplete for formatted values](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/issues/810), and one about the deprecated [django.conf.urls.url()](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/issues/831). I opened further issues on [attached documents in query results](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/issues/609) and on a [ForeignKey with unique=True](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-ps-timeseries/issues/11) in the timeseries plugin.

On the review side, I looked at several pull requests, including [ordering by fformatter](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/807), the [dependency bump](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/833) for the REST framework packages, [project links to OGC API](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-portal/pull/4), and [editing a feature from the attribute table](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/577).
