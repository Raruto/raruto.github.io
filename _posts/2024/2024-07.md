# More than just pretty icons

*Devlog: 2024-07*

In July I contributed to g3w-suite projects. I authored commits in [g3w-admin](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin), [g3w-client](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client), and [g3w-suite-docker](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-suite-docker), and also made commits in [g3w-admin-portal](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-portal) and [g3w-suite-demo-projects](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-suite-demo-projects). On the client side, I opened a pull request about [mouse cursors and map control icons](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/646).

I also opened an issue in g3w-admin proposing to [create a new project from a single zip file](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/issues/917).

Reviewing took up a good part of the month too. I reviewed client pull requests on [grouping the query-by controls into a single map control](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/647), [showing query, search and form content over the map](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/645), and [resetting nested search dependencies](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/643). I also looked at an [editing plugin change](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client-plugin-editing/pull/116) and two admin pull requests: [environment variables for the superuser](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/898) and a [join 1:1 fix](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/902).
