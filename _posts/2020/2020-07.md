# KMZ, Elevation, and UI Commits

*Devlog: 2020-07*

In July I contributed to [leaflet-kmz](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-kmz), [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation) and [leaflet-ui](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-ui), among other mapping repositories.

On the KMZ loader I worked on [missing icons in KML files](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-kmz/commit/e16d342148e19285b6d19395d2e93410644096c4) and [disabled tooltips on mobile](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-kmz/commit/5183d65f35c6a42599f11c4c2e9e1370e70a23c4). The [KMZ article](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-kmz) has examples of loading files; the image below instead illustrates the current elevation plugin.

The loader and profile control solve different problems, but both bring information from geographic files onto a map.
