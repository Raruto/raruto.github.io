# Another Month of Leaflet Elevation Commits

*Devlog: 2020-09*

In September I returned to [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation), a Leaflet plugin for displaying a track's elevation profile. The public history records an [update to the hoverable example](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/commit/0ec42352a4a929155b29d9fdb6119eb67dacbf5b), a dependency update and a change to [avoid global properties](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/commit/96826ed4cec04bb7b1217033882b825a2bf67bd9).

The [project retrospective]({{ '/blog/2018-11-02-leaflet-elevation-project.html' | relative_url }}) links to today's examples. The image below is a current illustration, not a screenshot of the September version.

It shows why the map and the profile belong together: each gives a different view of the same route.
