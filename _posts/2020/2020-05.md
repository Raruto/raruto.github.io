# An Alt Attribute for Event Images

*Contributions: 2020-05*

Looking back to May, my [WordPress.org activity feed](https://profiles.wordpress.org/raruto/feed/) records a [Plugins Trac ticket](https://plugins.trac.wordpress.org/ticket/2874) I opened about a missing `alt` attribute for the `#_EVENTIMAGE{x,y}` placeholder. A small piece of markup, but one worth reporting.

An image's [alternative text](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/img#alt) can stand in for the image when it isn't displayed, including for people using non-visual browsers. The public feed gives the ticket title and date; it doesn't establish the eventual outcome, so I won't guess at a fix.
