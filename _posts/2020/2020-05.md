# A Month with Leaflet Elevation

*Devlog: 2020-05*

In May I returned to [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation), the plugin that pairs a track's elevation profile with a map. Its history includes a [custom summary change](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/commit/cbc8cdae15973c9f50cd8407afc05edde5c0daa9) and work on a request to [translate the download label](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/issues/49).

Away from GitHub, the same month also appears in my WordPress activity: I opened a [ticket about an event-image attribute]({{ '/blog/2020-05-16-wordpress-event-images.html' | relative_url }}). The profile illustration below shows the plugin as it appears today.
