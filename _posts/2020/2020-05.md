# A Month with Leaflet Elevation

*Devlog: 2020-05*

In May I returned to [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation), which pairs a track's elevation profile with a map. I made a [custom summary change](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/commit/cbc8cdae15973c9f50cd8407afc05edde5c0daa9) and followed up on a request to [translate the download label](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/issues/49).

Away from GitHub, I opened a WordPress ticket about a missing `alt` attribute on the `#_EVENTIMAGE{x,y}` placeholder. Alternative text can make an image's content available when the image itself cannot be seen, including to people using non-visual browsers. The [ticket](https://plugins.trac.wordpress.org/ticket/2874) records the report, but doesn't establish its outcome.
