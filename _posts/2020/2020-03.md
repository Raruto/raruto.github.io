# When your chart has no data

*Devlog: 2020-03*

In March I contributed to [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation), [leaflet-google](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-google) and my [site](https://github.com/Raruto/raruto.github.io), among other repositories.

In the elevation plugin, I worked on [making the altitude legend localizable](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/commit/78960b9a89114e1401995b3c514c6c8181e0cdb3) and [handling empty data](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/commit/567bdfd9e540729f905e575caec5900bdbc9617c). The related [project article](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation) gives an overview of the plugin's current examples; the illustration here is current too.

One change concerns the text beside the chart; the other addresses what happens when there is no data to display.
