# A Few Loose Ends in BuddyPress Events

*Contributions: 2020-01*

Looking back to January, I shared a few proposals for the BuddyPress integration in Events Manager. One [patch](https://wordpress.org/support/topic/patch-custom-buddypress-navigation-slugs/) made navigation slugs easier to customize or translate; another [report](https://wordpress.org/support/topic/code-fix-buddypress-tab-title-invalid-string/) addressed an events-tab label that was getting in the way of translation.

I also suggested showing the [attending tab and profile information](https://wordpress.org/support/topic/code-fix-buddypress-attending-tab-conditional-loading/) only when bookings were enabled. The support team said they would pass the reports on to the developers. Around the same time, I shared some references in a [forum discussion about event duration](https://wordpress.org/support/topic/event-duration/#post-12305927).
