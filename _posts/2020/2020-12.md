# Cockpit Contributions: Translations, Paths, and Ideas

*Devlog: 2020-12*

In December I contributed to several Cockpit-related projects, including [CpMultiplane](https://github.com/raffaelj/CpMultiplane), its library skeleton, and Cockpit's translation files.

I also authored two commits in [agentejo/cockpit-i18n](https://github.com/agentejo/cockpit-i18n) and opened the [Update / missing translations](https://github.com/agentejo/cockpit-i18n/pull/35) pull request.

Toward the end of the month I opened CpMultiplane issues about a [Routes file](https://github.com/raffaelj/CpMultiplane/issues/31) and [PHPDoc blocks](https://github.com/raffaelj/CpMultiplane/issues/32).

CpMultiplane is a PHP frontend for Cockpit CMS. In November I had contributed an [Italian translation](https://github.com/raffaelj/CpMultiplane-i18n/pull/1), which was merged. My December fixes had a cross-platform angle: in the related library skeleton I replaced a `cp` command in a Composer script with PHP's `copy()` ([pull request](https://github.com/raffaelj/cpmp-lib-skeleton/pull/2)), and I fixed a [path expansion problem](https://github.com/raffaelj/CpMultiplane/pull/30) when loading translations on Windows. Both changes were merged.
