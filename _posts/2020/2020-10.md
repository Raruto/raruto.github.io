# Leaflet Elevation Commits Continue

*Devlog: 2020-10*

In October I contributed to [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation), where the work reached beyond altitude alone. One commit added an [acceleration graph](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/commit/59a4f0ab25b14f00fe8cce4966e327be89713f3f); others adjusted the [right axis styling](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/commit/b8fad241f678fdd6d1bb8c6527f21ced51cf22bd) and the [mouse focus label](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/commit/2ef94492d7eb5fa95d0633465589435e99900b18).

The result sits in the broader context of a plugin for viewing track data alongside a map. Its [project page]({{ '/blog/2018-11-02-leaflet-elevation-project.html' | relative_url }}) points to current examples, and the illustration below shows the current interface.
