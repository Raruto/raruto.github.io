# Leaflet Plugins and CpMultiplane Contributions

*Devlog: 2020-11*

November split between my Leaflet plugins and some contributions to the CpMultiplane ecosystem. Early on I made commits to [leaflet-pegman](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-pegman) and [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation). Later in the month I returned to leaflet-elevation, and also worked on [leaflet-ui](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-ui), [leaflet-gesture-handling](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-gesture-handling) and [leaflet-kmz](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-kmz).

![Illustration of leaflet-elevation](../img/leaflet-elevation.png)

*A current illustration of leaflet-elevation.*

On the CpMultiplane side, I opened a pull request with an [Italian translation](https://github.com/raffaelj/CpMultiplane-i18n/pull/1) and later another one to [rename the "it.php" language pack](https://github.com/raffaelj/CpMultiplane-i18n/pull/2). In [cpmp-lib-skeleton](https://github.com/raffaelj/cpmp-lib-skeleton) I opened issues about [Composer 2](https://github.com/raffaelj/cpmp-lib-skeleton/issues/1) and [local development](https://github.com/raffaelj/cpmp-lib-skeleton/issues/3). I also opened pull requests for a [cross-platform Composer "post-autoload-dump" script](https://github.com/raffaelj/cpmp-lib-skeleton/pull/2) and [BASE\_URL injection](https://github.com/raffaelj/cpmp-lib-skeleton/pull/4), and made commits there as well.
