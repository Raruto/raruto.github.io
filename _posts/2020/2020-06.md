# Slope on the map

*Devlog: 2020-06*

In June I contributed to [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation) and [leaflet-pegman](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-pegman), two plugins that approach a map from rather different perspectives.

For the elevation profile, I worked on the [summary generator](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/commit/cf8c52e79a2a10f517613cd702bf0362986e3c79) and a [missing slope label](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/commit/5b63de7152a18c3b1a8a492671cfeb640813ec3f). Pegman, meanwhile, connects Leaflet to Street View. The current illustration below shows the elevation side of that pairing.

There's more on the panorama control in the [Pegman project article](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-pegman).
