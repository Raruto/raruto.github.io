# Leaflet Google, wordpress-git, and an HTTPS Tiles Request

*Devlog: 2018-10*

In October I authored commits in [raruto.github.io](https://github.com/Raruto/raruto.github.io), [leaflet-google](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-google) and [wordpress-git](https://github.com/Raruto/wordpress-git).

![Illustration of leaflet-google](../img/leaflet-google.jpg)

*A current illustration of leaflet-google.*

Before that, on the 24th, I opened an issue on [openptmap](https://github.com/giggls/openptmap/issues/2) about streaming its tiles over HTTPS / SSL.

The map-side work included [leaflet-google]({{ '/blog/2018-10-31-leaflet-google-project.html' | relative_url }}), a plugin connecting Leaflet with the Google Maps API. I also made changes to the site repository that contains my earlier [maps playground]({{ '/blog/2018-10-25-maps-playground.html' | relative_url }}). The illustration shows the plugin as it appears today, not necessarily its October version.
