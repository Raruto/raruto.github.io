# Reviewing Client Plugin Features and WMS Legend Bugs

*Devlog: 2023-06*

In June I authored commits in [g3w-admin-portal](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-portal) and also authored commits in [g3w-client](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client) and the [demo projects](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-suite-demo-projects) repository.

On the client side, I reviewed a batch of pull requests, including [external download providers for plugins](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/434), [partial kmz NetworkLink support](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/430), [exposing interaction controls to third-party plugins](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/428), and [preventing click-to-select on external WMS layers](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/432). I also reviewed a few editing-plugin changes, such as the [\`listenChangeInput\` guard restore](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/436).

I opened two issues in g3w-client: one about an [indeterminate checkbox state in treeview items](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/issues/423) and another about a [WMS legend click error](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/issues/431). I also opened an issue on [togeojson about GPX metadataType](https://github.com/placemark/togeojson/issues/111).
