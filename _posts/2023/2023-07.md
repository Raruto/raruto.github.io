# GUI Refactoring Proposals, Factories and Event Bus

*Devlog: 2023-07*

In July I contributed to [g3w-client](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client). I opened a run of refactoring pull requests, including one to [refactor the GUI class](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/451), another to [delete the legend folder](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/455), and one to [add a shared eventbus module](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/458). I also opened pull requests to [refactor LayerFactory](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/449) and [ProviderFactory](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/456), and one to [move g3w-ol into mapservice](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/459). Earlier in the month I reviewed a few pull requests there, such as the one on [Vue warnings in the development build](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/446).

I also authored commits in a few other g3w-suite repositories: [g3w-admin-portal](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-portal), [g3w-admin-authjwt](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-authjwt) and [g3w-suite-docker](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-suite-docker).

On the Leaflet side, I committed to [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation) and [leaflet-rotate](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate), and reviewed a [leaflet-rotate pull request](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-rotate/pull/34) about duplicated event listeners on draggable markers.
