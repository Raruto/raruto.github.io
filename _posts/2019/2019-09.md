# Elevation, UI, and Site Commits

*Devlog: 2019-09*

In September I contributed to [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation), [leaflet-ui](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-ui) and my [site](https://github.com/Raruto/raruto.github.io), among other repositories.

The elevation history includes [changes to prevent a detached chart overlapping the map](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/commit/97116582f68ea4ed1d73d365196857205598c6b0) and a [refactoring of the download logic](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/commit/6502c7432e4b1f39ea38d50cc1146be2f25fdce7). Those details sit alongside the bigger picture: a plugin for reading a track's profile and a separate collection of map-interface presets.

The [elevation article]({{ '/blog/2018-11-02-leaflet-elevation-project.html' | relative_url }}) connects that history to the project's current examples.
