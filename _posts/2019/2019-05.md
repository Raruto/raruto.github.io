# Leaflet Plugin Commits and Issues Across Several Projects

*Devlog: 2019-05*

In May I authored commits in [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation), [raruto.github.io](https://github.com/Raruto/raruto.github.io), [leaflet-kmz](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-kmz), [leaflet-pegman](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-pegman) and [leaflet-transparency](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-transparency).

![Illustration of leaflet-elevation](../img/leaflet-elevation.png)

*A current illustration of leaflet-elevation.*

I also opened a few issues along the way. In leaflet-elevation I filed one about [loading multiple gpx files](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/issues/7) and another about [QGIS integration](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/issues/5). In leaflet-kmz I reported that [marker icons are cut on the edges of map canvas tiles](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-kmz/issues/2). I also suggested [adding some fading-out to the warning overlay](https://github.com/elmarquis/Leaflet.GestureHandling/issues/40) in Leaflet.GestureHandling, and wrote up [My Two Cents](https://github.com/faunalia/trackprofile2web/issues/2) for faunalia/trackprofile2web. At the end of the month I authored a couple of commits in that same [trackprofile2web](https://github.com/faunalia/trackprofile2web) repository.

The month's work also included [leaflet-transparency](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-transparency), a Leaflet version of the opacity control I had first explored in [google-transparency]({{ '/blog/2018-11-22-google-transparency-project.html' | relative_url }}). Its [demo](https://raruto.github.io/leaflet-transparency/examples/leaflet-transparency.html) shows a slider for adjusting how much of an overlay remains visible over the base map.
