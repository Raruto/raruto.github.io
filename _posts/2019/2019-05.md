# Turning Down the Opacity

*Projects: 2019-05*

My [leaflet-transparency](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-transparency) repository dates back to May. Its opacity control lets a Leaflet map reveal more or less of a layer with a slider.

The project began from the earlier [google-transparency]({{ '/blog/2018-11-22-google-transparency-project.html' | relative_url }}) control. The [demo](https://raruto.github.io/leaflet-transparency/examples/leaflet-transparency.html) shows how a little transparency can make comparing map layers less of an all-or-nothing choice.

The control puts that adjustment on the map itself, so the visible balance can change without replacing the underlying layers. The screenshot below shows the interface rather than a historical release.
