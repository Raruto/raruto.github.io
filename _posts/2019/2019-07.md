# Gesture Handling, Elevation, and wordpress-git Commits

*Devlog: 2019-07*

In July I contributed to [leaflet-gesture-handling](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-gesture-handling), [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation) and [wordpress-git](https://github.com/Raruto/wordpress-git).

On the elevation plugin, one [change introduced a shared layer object and addressed clearing GPX layers](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/commit/0d7e9ba340721ab2898db358feba344185bf05be). The gesture-handling plugin tackles a different part of a map: how scrolling and touch gestures interact with the surrounding page.
