# A Leaflet-Focused April: Pegman, Elevation, and KMZ

*Devlog: 2019-04*

April was a Leaflet-heavy month for me. On the 7th I made commits to [leaflet-pegman](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-pegman) and [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation), two Leaflet plugins.

![Illustration of leaflet-elevation](../img/leaflet-elevation.png)

*A current illustration of leaflet-elevation.*

On the 15th I shifted my attention to [leaflet-kmz](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-kmz), where I authored a batch of commits and opened an issue titled [Author's notes](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-kmz/issues/1). I also made some commits to my [GitHub Pages repository](https://github.com/Raruto/raruto.github.io) that same day.

The three plugins approach maps from different angles: [Pegman]({{ '/blog/2018-11-19-leaflet-pegman-project.html' | relative_url }}) connects to Street View, [Elevation]({{ '/blog/2018-11-02-leaflet-elevation-project.html' | relative_url }}) displays track profiles, and [KMZ]({{ '/blog/2019-04-14-leaflet-kmz-project.html' | relative_url }}) brings KML and KMZ data into Leaflet. The current README documents native browser decompression for supported KMZ archives and examples of [KMZ layers](https://raruto.github.io/leaflet-kmz/examples/leaflet-kmz.html) and [vector grids](https://raruto.github.io/leaflet-kmz/examples/leaflet-kmz_gridlayer.html). It credits [A-Lurker](https://github.com/a-lurker/leaflet-kmz) and [BrandonXiang](https://github.com/brandonxiang/leaflet-geojson-vt) among its contributors.
