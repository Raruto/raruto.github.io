# Elevation, KMZ, and Site Commits

*Devlog: 2019-11*

# From Looking at a Map to Editing It

*Projects: 2019-11*

A map can also be a starting point for editing. My [leaflet-edit-osm](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-edit-osm) repository, created in November, adds an unobtrusive link for opening the map area in an OpenStreetMap editor.

The project was initially based on [Tom MacWright's work](https://github.com/osmlab/leaflet-edit-osm). The [demo](https://raruto.github.io/leaflet-edit-osm/examples/leaflet-edit-osm.html) shows the control in its natural habitat: right on the map.

The README documents a choice of editors in the control's options. It's a small bridge between browsing geographic data and opening the same area for a possible contribution.
