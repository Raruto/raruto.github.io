# Elevation, KMZ, and Site Commits

*Devlog: 2019-11*

In November I contributed to [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation), [leaflet-kmz](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-kmz) and my [site](https://github.com/Raruto/raruto.github.io), among other repositories.

For elevation profiles, I added an [altitude legend toggle and interpolation for missing elevation values](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/commit/eb30bca2d0dfb7367e4e7606c3bf6d0e053cec30), followed by a [legend option and redraw function](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation/commit/2c1703c103fea6ab24d714539bae9446ab8a64b7). The profile in the current illustration below gives those changes a place on the map.

The [project retrospective]({{ '/blog/2018-11-02-leaflet-elevation-project.html' | relative_url }}) has more examples of tracks and profiles.

I also created [leaflet-edit-osm](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-edit-osm), a small control that opens the visible map area in an OpenStreetMap editor. Based initially on [Tom MacWright's work](https://github.com/osmlab/leaflet-edit-osm), it lets users choose an editor in the control options; the [demo](https://raruto.github.io/leaflet-edit-osm/examples/leaflet-edit-osm.html) shows it on the map.
