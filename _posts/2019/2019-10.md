# Leaflet Trails and UI Commits

*Devlog: 2019-10*

In October I worked across [leaflet-trails](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-trails), [leaflet-ui](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-ui) and my [site](https://github.com/Raruto/raruto.github.io), among other repositories.

The trails history includes an [update to examples](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-trails/commit/f637d05acb543c424f9f517b7249edd46922efbb) and a [label-width adjustment](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-trails/commit/d0e6f1cbc7e254425827beb70771395f8b89b95d). The plugin brings Waymarked Trails overlays into Leaflet; the [dedicated article]({{ '/blog/2019-06-07-leaflet-trails-project.html' | relative_url }}) links to more of its examples. The image below is a current illustration.

Those overlays sit on top of a base map, keeping the routes and their geographic context together.
