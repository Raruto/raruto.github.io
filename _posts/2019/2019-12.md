# Italian Translation Files for Horos

*Devlog: 2019-12*

On December 24, 2019, I opened a [pull request for Italian translation files](https://github.com/horosproject/horos/pull/526) in [Horos](https://github.com/horosproject/horos), a DICOM medical-image viewer.

In the description I noted that I had translated `Localizible.strings` and added the other files needed in the `it-IT.lproj` folder, then asked whether anything was missing. The pull request remains unmerged; it is a record of the proposed localization, not a claim that the translation shipped with Horos.
