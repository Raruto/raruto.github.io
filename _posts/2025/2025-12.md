# Reviewing Missing Alphanumerical Layers in Metadata

*Devlog: 2025-12*

On December 22 I reviewed a [g3w-client pull request](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/879) about alphanumerical layers missing from the metadata layers tab. The author included before-and-after screenshots in the discussion, making the missing entries and the proposed result easier to compare.

My contribution here was the review, not authorship of the fix. The pull request was merged on January 5, 2026; the [original discussion](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/879) has the screenshots and subsequent history.
