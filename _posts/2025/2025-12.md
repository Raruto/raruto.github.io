# Reviewing Missing Alphanumerical Layers in Metadata

*Devlog: 2025-12*

On December 22 I reviewed a [g3w-client pull request](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/879) about alphanumerical layers missing from the metadata layers tab.
