# i18next and EventEmitter Removal Proposals

*Devlog: 2025-07*

In July I authored commits in [g3w-client](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client), [g3w-admin-portal](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-portal), [g3w-admin](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin), [g3w-suite-docker](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-suite-docker) and [g3w-client-plugin-editing](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client-plugin-editing) as well.

On the client, I opened several pull requests: one to [remove the i18next package](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/818), one on [Vue i18n reactivity](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/825), and one to [remove the EventEmitter dependency](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/826). I also opened a pull request to [remove some editing APIs from core](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/828). Two more covered [loading external layers with unregistered EPSGs](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/824) and [switching base layers with a Bing base layer lacking a vendorkey](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/823).

I also reviewed other pull requests, including a [Bulgarian translation](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/821), a [legend filtering change](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/832), and a [sidebar toggler fix for iframes](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/836).
