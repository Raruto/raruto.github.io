# Recallable Messages, Branch Manager and Elasticsearch Geocoding

*Devlog: 2025-05*

In May I authored commits in [g3w-admin](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin), [g3w-client](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client), and the [editing plugin](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client-plugin-editing). On the pull request side, I opened [Recallable Messages/Info](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/1117) for the admin, alongside its [client counterpart](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/802). I also opened a [client branch manager (beta)](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/1116) and an [annotation map control](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/1102) for the admin. On the client, I opened a [new "qes" geocoding provider](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/806), the Elasticsearch-backed one.

I also reviewed a good number of pull requests. These included the [admin's "qes" Django module](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/1101), [limiting fields in query results previews](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/797), [filtering the legend by map content](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/799), and [handling layer selection on paginated tables](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/804). Other reviews covered [customizable layer visibility constraints](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/1113) and [Qplotly widget ordering](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin/pull/1115).
