# Rogue relations & Projection quirks

*Devlog: 2025-10*

In October I reviewed pull requests across g3w-suite projects. In [g3w-client](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client), I reviewed changes covering [the form view field visualization fix](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/873), [cascade 1:N relations](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/870), [alphanumeric layer styling](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/868), and [annotation text color](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/862). I also looked at [an empty-result bug when querying layers with a different projection](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/860), [the French translation update](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/859), and [annotation tool labels not updating](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client/pull/858).

The editing plugin got some review attention too, including [adding a feature from the relations tab](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client-plugin-editing/pull/176), [saving a feature with null geometry](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client-plugin-editing/pull/174), and [the Changes modal fix](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-client-plugin-editing/pull/173). On the processing side, I reviewed [a French translation](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-processing/pull/22).

I also authored commits in [g3w-admin-portal](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-portal), [g3w-admin-processing](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin-processing), and [g3w-admin](https://github.com/g3w-suite/g3w-admin), plus a couple in [leaflet-elevation](https://github.com/Raruto/leaflet-elevation).
